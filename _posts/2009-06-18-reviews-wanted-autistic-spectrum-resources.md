<p>It seems like every time I open my email or web browser I come across more &#8220;resources&#8221; for parents with children on the spectrum.</p>
<p>I&#8217;m concerned, however, that there&#8217;s almost too many, if such a thing is possible, because much of the information is scattered, duplicated, and even contradictory. What the community might need is a way to cross-reference and evaluate the broad spectrum of resources to better meet a particular challenge.</p>
<p>So, with no real order to this list other than referrals and the results of various searches, here&#8217;s a list of resources, mostly non-profit organizations, for which we&#8217;re specifically seeking reviews. We&#8217;ll integrate those reviews into both a guest post on the resource as well as into the reviews section of the budding social network.</p>
