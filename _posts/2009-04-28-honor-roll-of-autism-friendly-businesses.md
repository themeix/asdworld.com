<p>I&#8217;m writing this post now because I just received a message from <a href="https://www.riminiautismo.it/">Rimini Autismo</a>, an Italian organization, and they have an &#8220;<a href="https://www.riminiautismo.it/autismfriendly.htm">autism friendly</a>&#8221; page.</p>
<p>Please put names and contact information about businesses you have come across that are friendly towards those on the spectrum and their families. I&#8217;ll create a separate page recognizing them and your stories about them. (And after the re-design, we&#8217;ll migrate all those comments to a permanent place.)</p>
<p>So add your story about a favorite autism-friendly business in the comments!</p>
