<p>As the first public activity of the Wolfhound Fund, we will be sponsoring a screening of Jackie Spirer&#8217;s film &#8220;Finding Matty&#8217;s Voice.&#8221; Her <a href="https://sites.allegheny.edu/news/releases/recent_allegheny_grad_jaclyn_spirer_wins_top_honor_at_ivy_film_festival.php">film</a> won the <a href="https://old.ivyfilmfestival.com/news/winners-2008-ivy-film-festival">Grand Jury Prize</a> at this year&#8217;s <a href="https://old.ivyfilmfestival.com/home">Ivy Film Festival</a>.
