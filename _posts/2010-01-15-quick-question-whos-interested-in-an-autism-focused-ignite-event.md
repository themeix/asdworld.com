<p>&#8220;Ignite&#8221; talks are presentations composed of 15 slides that automatically rotate every 20 seconds, meaning they&#8217;re only 5 minutes long.</p>
<p>Since I&#8217;ve been working on a similarly sized presentation to introduce my ideas on a comprehensive ASD curriculum planning &amp; tracking tool, and since March 1-4 is <a href="https://igniteshow.com/">Global Ignite Week</a>, I wondered whether there are others in our community who would like to present a short presentation in this style.</p>
<p>There&#8217;s certainly more that can be done, but perhaps some focus might be an interesting way to get ideas on the table and excite each other about what we&#8217;re thinking in the background.</p>
<p>Please leave a comment if you&#8217;re interested in presenting or helping organize such an event. If there are only a few of us, perhaps we&#8217;ll piggyback on some NYC events that same week.</p>
