<p>After weeks with no response from the Christie campaign on my question about the candidate&#8217;s position on autism (I guess he believes twitter is for press releases rather than conversations), I looked over NJ Gov&#8217;r Corzine&#8217;s scant website. No presence for autism there either; in fact, neither of these websites contained a search box. I ended up using google to [fruitlessly] search <a href="https://www.google.co.uk/search?hl=en&amp;as_q=&amp;as_epq=&amp;as_oq=autism+spectrum+%22special+education%22&amp;as_eq=&amp;num=10&amp;lr=&amp;as_filetype=&amp;as_sitesearch=christiefornj.com&amp;as_qdr=all&amp;as_rights=&amp;as_occt=any&amp;cr=&amp;as_nlo=&amp;as_nhi=&amp;safe=off&amp;gws_rd=cr,ssl&amp;ei=HHxPVcvEKYrzoATvtYHgCA">Christie&#8217;s</a> website and <a href="https://www.google.co.uk/search?hl=en&amp;as_q=&amp;as_epq=&amp;as_oq=autism+spectrum+%22special+education%22&amp;as_eq=&amp;num=10&amp;lr=&amp;as_filetype=&amp;as_sitesearch=joncorzine09.com/&amp;as_qdr=all&amp;as_rights=&amp;as_occt=any&amp;cr=&amp;as_nlo=&amp;as_nhi=&amp;safe=off&amp;gws_rd=cr,ssl&amp;ei=OXxPVZf7OYbcoATygoCwCQ">Corzine&#8217;s</a>. (&#8220;Corruption&#8221; has 36 entries on Christie&#8217;s site &#8212; politics may really be about the past.)</p>
<p>Since Christie&#8217;s site doesn&#8217;t have a way to ask a question, I sent the below email through Corzine&#8217;s site:</p>
<blockquote><p>Dear Gov. Corzine,</p>
<p>I&#8217;m a parent of a young boy with autism (<a href="https://www.cbsnews.com/videos/congress-debates-autism-bill/">https://www.tinyurl.com/DylanCBS</a> &#8211; clip from CBS&#8217;s The Early Show). We have too many friends for whom autism has taken over their lives.</p>
<p>What&#8217;s your position on helping NJ parents with autism, who are more affected than in any other state?</p>
<p>Hint: the words &#8220;autism&#8221; &#8220;spectrum&#8221; and &#8220;special education&#8221; don&#8217;t appear on Chris Christie&#8217;s website.</p>
<p>I&#8217;ve been a longtime libertarian, but autism is making me into a single-issue voter.</p>
<p>Thanks,</p>
<p>Rick Colosimo</p></blockquote>
<p>What do you think? Are parents of autistic children (including young adults and those who are adults) going to become single-issue voters? For the acid test, would you vote for a candidate who had your views on autism but differed on welfare, immigration, or even abortion?</p>
