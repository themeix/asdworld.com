<p>Readers of Exaptation will note that a recent post dealt with Long-Term Potentiation, or LTP. LTP is related to the manner in which signals travel in the brain (very simplified explanation!).</p>
<p>This <a href="https://www.molecularmovies.com/showcase/#Neuronal%20Signaling">animation</a> of LTP explains the process in much greater detail. Other visualizations relevant to understanding some of the scientific research regarding autism include:</p>
<p></p>
<ul>
<li><a href="https://www.dnai.org/index.htm">DNAinteractive</a> has a variety of visualizations and learning materials on DNA, but the section on the &#8220;<a href="https://www.dnai.org/a/index.html">Code</a>&#8221; is the best introduction to understanding how molecular biologists and geneticists think about DNA</li>
<li>this section of <a href="https://www.molecularmovies.com/showcase/#Transcription">Transcription</a> animations attempts to illuminate one part of how we go from DNA to proteins</li>
</ul>
<p>Admittedly, these are complicated topics, but it&#8217;s important to educate ourselves a bit so that we can appropriately assess research, or the lack thereof, on theories of causation, treatment, and cures for autism.</p>
