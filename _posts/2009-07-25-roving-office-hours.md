<p>Earlier today, in a <a href="https://groups.yahoo.com/neo/groups/Mosaic-List/conversations/topics/48820">post</a> on <a href="https://groups.yahoo.com/neo/groups/Mosaic-List/info">MOSAIC</a>, the NJ autism parents&#8217; group, I referenced my last <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/results-not-efforts-should-drive-placements/">post</a>, in which I talked about rewriting actual IEP goals as one project I&#8217;d like to undertake (along with so many others!).</p>
<p>To make it easier to discuss these issues, and to help parents find a more convenient forum to ask their questions, I&#8217;m instituting a series of &#8220;Roving Office Hours&#8221; at Starbucks locations around northern NJ.</p>
<p>The first two will take place in <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/roving-office-hours-midland-park/">Midland Park</a> on August 3 and <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/roving-office-hours-montclair/">Montclair</a> on August 5. Here&#8217;s the automatically updating page for <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/?m=200908&amp;cat=47">events in August</a>.</p>
<p>Parents can stop by, ask questions, and get help with writing IEP goals, guidance on general strategy, and even just some friendly conversation.</p>
<p>If you want me to schedule a session near your location, please send <a href="https://twitter.com/rickcolosimo">me </a>times and dates that you think will work well.</p>
