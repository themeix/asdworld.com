<p>Summer is well past over, and one hurricane, one Fall season, and a few major holidays later, there&#8217;s finally some time to think about it.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I&#8217;d like to say thanks to the <a href="https://ridgewoodsoccer.org">Ridgewood Soccer Association</a>, which runs a special needs soccer program alongside the large program for typical kids in town.</p>
<p>What made this special for me, and my sons, is that they could both participate in the same sport and share many of the same experiences while still having a good time appropriate for each of them.</p>
<p>Here&#8217;s a <a href="https://ridgewoodsoccer.org/2012/12/04/special-season-ends-with-hugs/">brief rundown of the season</a>, including lots of extra gratitude for the RHS women&#8217;s soccer players who helped out every Saturday. Dylan&#8217;s in the grey shirt in the last picture!</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Knowing that my community &#8212; not just the school district &#8212; cares about my son in this very visible way means an awful lot to me. I really like this town.</p>
