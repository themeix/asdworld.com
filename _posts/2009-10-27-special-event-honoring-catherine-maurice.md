<p>Every parent of a child on the spectrum knows the name Catherine Maurice. Her book, &#8220;<a href="https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0449906647?ie=UTF8&amp;tag=thoughtstorm&amp;linkCode=as2&amp;camp=1789&amp;creative=390957&amp;creativeASIN=0449906647">Let Me Hear Your Voice</a>,&#8221; is a literary landmark in the field that has touched almost every parent I know.</p>
<p>In a rare public appearance made even more rare because it&#8217;s parent-driven, Catherine Maurice will be honored by <a href="https://www.reedacademy.org/">REED Academy</a> at their <a href="https://www.reedacademy.org/events.htm">7th Annual Fall Night Out</a>. The theme this year is Denim &amp; Diamonds [invitation &#8211; PDF],  bringing a more festive atmosphere to the gala.</p>
<h3>What&#8217;s it going to be like?</h3>
<p>In addition to a sit-down dinner, full open bar, live music, beautiful view, complimentary valet parking, a coveted guest speaker, cool live jazz, and the opportunity to go out in your best jeans and have a good time, we have numerous exclusive auction items that you can&#8217;t just buy anywhere.</p>
<p><em>Dance in a Broadway show.  Take a break from the cold this winter in the Bahamas.  Surprise the family with tickets to the Nickelodeon Kids&#8217; Choice Awards.  Walk the Red Carpet and attend a private party at a New York premiere of Wall Street 2.  It&#8217;ll all be there!</em></p>
<p>(I&#8217;ll post a sampling of the auction items at the bottom of this post.)</p>

<p>The event is on Saturday night, November 14th, from 7 pm to 11 pm at the <a href="https://www.newarkclub.com/directions.html">Newark Club</a> in Newark, NJ.</p>
