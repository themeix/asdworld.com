<p>I just came across <a href="https://www.friendshipcircle.org/blog/2012/03/07/how-to-teach-chores-to-your-special-needs-child/">this article</a> about a boy whose family has integrated him into family chores in the classic ABA model: harnessing natural motivations, creating discrete tasks, and implementing positive reinforcement, both tangible and social.</p>
<p>My older son is 8, and he&#8217;s certainly capable of many things. But I haven&#8217;t formalized the chores for him or my younger son, 6. Perhaps it&#8217;s time to do that. (And yes, I believe that self-care leads to self-reliance, which is the core of self-advocacy.)</p>
<p>I think dishes and the dishwasher, vacuuming, and picking up clothes as a precursor to laundry (have to install a washer/dryer in the new loft apartment we just moved into). I know that older kids at <a href="https://www.reedacademy.org/">Reed Academy</a> have learned to do laundry</p>
<p> </p>
<p>What chores do your kids do? And at what ages?</p>
