<p>My fees for legal services are $300 per hour, but there is some variation dependent on <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/services/">services</a> &amp; <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/i-need-iep-help/">location</a>.</p>
<p>Initial consultation: I regularly provide an initial consultation, over the phone, of up to 15 minutes for free. The actual call will take longer since I have to gather information from you and provide some required <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/services/disclosures/">disclosures</a>.</p>
<p>What is covered?</p>
<ol>
<li>All activity on your matter
<ol>
<li>Phone calls, email, and letters</li>
<li>Record reviews – generally we can discuss a flat fee once I see the state of your records [[make link to record-keeping page]] or the use of a junior person to organize your records at a greatly reduced fee</li>
<li>Meetings</li>
<li>Hearings</li>
<li>Travel time, except that I do not charge for local travel time (under ½ hour actual one-way from my office) or for time commuting into Manhattan from NJ for a meeting in Manhattan.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li>Expenses are additional, charged at my actual cost
<ol>
<li>No charges for domestic phone calls, faxes, or US mail.</li>
<li>No charges for local travel, as described above</li>
</ol>
</li>
</ol>
<p>Retainer: a retainer in the amount of 4-10 hours of work is required, which we can discuss during the initial consultation.</p>
