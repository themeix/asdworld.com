<h3>Do you need help?</h3>
<p>You should not hesitate to contact me if you need assistance, especially if you have received a letter from the district that is unexpected or proposes dramatic changes for your child. There are certain IDEA rules that trigger a short 15-day deadline before things happen automatically to your child&#8217;s placement.</p>
